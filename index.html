    • Frequently Asked Questions

    Who should attend DEVCONF 2026?

    DEVCONF is designed for engineers, founders, product leaders, and technical teams who want to explore AI, cloud, frontend, security, and modern software engineering.

    What is included with my ticket?

    Every pass includes access to all conference talks, networking areas, community sessions, coffee breaks, and expo hall access. Premium passes also include workshops and VIP experiences.

    Are there student or team discounts?

    Yes. We offer team pricing for groups of up to 10 attendees and limited student/early-career support options while availability lasts.

    Can I transfer or refund my registration?

    Registrations can be transferred to another attendee up to 14 days before the event. Refunds are available within the early-bird window and follow our event policy.
